## Observations (descriptive; no model fitted, no trial removed)

- At the P2 payload (6144 tokens, 46 blocks, 46,103,040 B per reader) one READ completes in 0.964 ms (1 active pair) or 0.971 ms (8 active pairs), median over all trials. Per-reader throughput is 47.4-48.8 GB/s, slightly higher at more requests in flight. For comparison, the formal H800 pair (126.2 Gbit/s, 0.0647 ms) prices the same READ at 2.987 ms.
- Completion grows in proportion to bytes: 16 requests in flight take 15.12-15.16 ms; 6145/6143 = 1.10798 against the byte ratio 1.10870.
- Equal-byte 6143 and 6144 agree: paired median differences -0.0014 to +0.0022 ms.
- Pass repeatability: spread of the three pass medians is 0.090% at the median case and 0.882% at the largest (`t6143_pairs8_inflight4`).
- Tails: 12 of 90 case-passes have one trial above 3x their median (largest 19.94 ms against a 1.918 ms median, `t6144_pairs8_inflight2` pass 2); the heaviest p90 is 14.979 ms against an 8.411 ms median (`t6145_pairs8_inflight8` pass 1). The largest single reader trial is 27.497 ms (`t6145_pairs8_inflight16`), above the 24.38 ms minimum P2-64 next-step gap; whether the P2 replays reach that concurrency is the Q2 question.

## Limits

- Standalone synthetic NIXL READ on 2x8 H200 with synthetic block selection; it reproduces the packed-block geometry but does not observe native serving request wire bytes (the separate payload edge for YC).
- It does not reproduce full P2 serving contention.
- Container exit codes are inferred from replica state because pod reads are forbidden for the personal credential.
- No timing model or criterion is adopted by this report; Q2 proposes them.
